Built on fire,
finished with care.

What started as a single wood-fired roaster in a converted garage is now a neighbourhood ritual. We travel to the farms we buy from, roast in batches small enough to taste the difference, and treat every pour-over like it's the only one we'll make that day.

No shortcuts, no syrups pretending to be flavour — just honest beans, honest heat, and people who care where their morning comes from.

— The Ember & Oak Roasting Team

From Farm to Cup

Four steps we never rush.

01

Origin

Sourced directly from single-origin farms across Ethiopia, Colombia and Sumatra.

02

Roast

Slow roasted in small batches over an open-flame drum for depth, not bitterness.

03

Grind

Ground fresh to order, calibrated to the exact brew method you choose.

04

Brew

Extracted with precision and poured with the same intention every single time.
